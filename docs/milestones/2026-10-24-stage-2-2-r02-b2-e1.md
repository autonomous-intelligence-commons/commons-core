## 4. Security remediation record

### S1 — Custody uncertainty

When Fake Provider custody evidence is insufficient following loss/restart, the effect remains conservatively **UNKNOWN/inconclusive** rather than being promoted to a definitive failure that might permit an unsafe retry.

### S2 — Originating attempt provenance

Delayed acknowledgments retain the originating `claim_epoch` and `claim_owner` instead of deriving them from a newer authoritative row state. Explicitly stale epoch evidence must be rejected.

### S3 — Complete ACK owner fencing

`apply_provider_ack()` now fails closed when either the ACK epoch or owner is absent; it does not substitute the row's current owner for missing provenance. Owner and epoch are validated before successful-state idempotency and other terminal-state shortcuts. Correct same-attempt duplicate ACKs retain idempotent behavior. Conditional database transition (CAS) predicates are preserved.

A provenance-missing ACK has a per-effect deduplicated audit marker. This is **not** a general-purpose concurrency-safe audit rate limiter, and an E2 external ACK channel would require renewed design review.
